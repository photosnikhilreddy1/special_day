First Date – Windmill Whispers 20 June

A windmill turning slow.

Our first beer.

Your eyes speaking quietly to mine.

A comfort I didn’t expect.

A spark I didn’t understand yet.

The bike ride after—

cool air, soft silence, shared space.

Your hand on my shoulder—warm, gentle, grounding.

A touch that stayed in my mind long after.

And somewhere inside,

a small wish began—

a simple side hug, someday.

A tiny beginning.

A quiet start.

But it was the start of us.
